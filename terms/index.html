1. Regulatory & Partner Disclosure

SendMoni is a financial technology application, not a commercial bank. Payment collection, card acquiring, bank account resolution, and payout settlement services are provided through licensed PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processors Paystack and Flutterwave.

2. Double-Currency Wallet Architecture

Every registered user is provisioned strictly two digital wallets based on their Country of Residence: (1) their country's official Local Currency Wallet and (2) a USD Foreign Currency Wallet (or GBP for residents of the United States and USD-based jurisdictions), along with a unique 10-digit SendMoni Account Number and @tag.

3. Transparent Fee Schedule

  • SendMoni-to-SendMoni App Transfer (Same Currency): 0.0% fee
  • SendMoni-to-SendMoni App Transfer (Cross-Currency FX): 0.3% FX spread
  • International Bank & Mobile Money Payout: 0.5% FX spread
  • Local Commercial Bank Transfer (Same Currency): 0.0% FX conversion fee
  • One-Tap Bill Reversal (Airtime / Data / Electricity): 100% full refund to Local Currency Wallet
  • Bill-to-Cash Converter (Airtime / Data / Electricity): 90% instant cash payout (10% conversion fee)
