SendMoni Privacy Policy & Data Safety

← Back to SendMoni

Effective Date: November 2026 · Compliant with GDPR, NDPR, CCPA, Apple App Store & Google Play User Data Policies

1. Information We Collect

SendMoni collects your legal full name, email address, phone number, country of residence, and optional KYC identity document strictly to provision your Double-Currency Digital Wallets (Local Currency + USD/GBP), assign your unique 10-digit SendMoni Account Number and @tag, verify identity under Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations, and execute money transfers and utility bill payments.

2. Payment Card & Bank Processing (Paystack & Flutterwave)

SendMoni never stores raw credit/debit card numbers, PINs, or CVV security codes on its servers. All card deposits, bank transfers, USSD collections, and mobile money transactions are processed directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified licensed payment gateway partners Paystack and Flutterwave.

3. Data Encryption & Mobile Hardware Enclave

SendMoni operates a Mobile-Only transaction architecture. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and authentication tokens are stored inside your mobile device's hardware-backed Secure Storage (Keychain / Keystore). We never sell or rent user data to third-party advertisers.

4. Account & Personal Data Deletion Rights

You may permanently delete your SendMoni account, wallets, and personal data at any time directly inside the mobile app (Vault / Settings → Delete My Account & Personal Data) or via our web deletion portal at /delete-account.

5. Contact & Data Protection Desk

For privacy inquiries, data subject access requests, or regulatory compliance questions, contact: support@paynora.com
